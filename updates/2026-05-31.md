# [Week of 2026-05-31] — Spec complete for the final MVP feature.

---

This week I finished speccing out Feature 003 — Facebook Video Upload. That's the last feature FieldKit needs to call MVP done.

The flow: a small business owner approves a video via Telegram. FieldKit picks up the approval, uploads the video to their Facebook Page, and sends back a confirmation with a link to the live post. One tap. No manual posting.

To get there, I ran the full spec-kit workflow — spec, clarifications, plan, sequence diagram, tasks, Gherkin acceptance tests — before writing a single line of code. Five questions surfaced decisions that would have bitten me mid-implementation: whether to require a caption (no, deferred), how many Facebook Pages per client (one), where to store the auth token (.env), and what the retry policy looks like (3 attempts, 60-second intervals).

The spec is done. The implementation starts next.

When Feature 003 ships, FieldKit will be able to take a photo, generate a video, get human approval, and post it to Facebook — end to end, automatically.
